Disclaimer - Indicative CO₂ footprint

The CO₂ footprint calculated by Carbon Compass is an indicative estimate based on the information provided by the user, standard emission factors and certain underlying assumptions. The results are intended for general awareness and preliminary assessment of individual carbon footprints. Actual emissions may vary depending on consumption patterns, geographical location, energy sources, technology, emission factors and other variables. While reasonable efforts are made to ensure the reliability of the calculations, Carbon Compass does not guarantee the absolute accuracy, completeness or precision of the results. These estimates should not be construed as a substitute for a detailed carbon audit, verified GHG inventory, regulatory disclosure or professional environmental assessment.

The results are intended to help users understand their carbon footprint and identify opportunities for reducing emissions..
